---
name: Liv research source policy
description: Source allowlisting and reuse rules for product research in Liv.
---

Only treat catalog data and web sources from approved domains as evidence. Web-search results are transient: do not persist them unless the source's reuse rights are recorded and permit caching. Mark community-submitted records as unverified until checked against an authoritative source; never derive numeric product scores from unsupported claims.

**Why:** Third-party search results can have unclear reuse rights, and inaccurate product labels or unsupported scores could mislead users.

**How to apply:** Keep an approved-domain registry for search, preserve source and license metadata for cached facts, and require verification before using a product fact in scores or recommendations.
