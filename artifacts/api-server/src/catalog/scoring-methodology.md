# Liv scoring methodology: skincare pilot

Scores are dimension-specific and independent. Liv does not compute an overall product score.

## Current pilot status

The pilot source set contains community-submitted product records and a limited number of linked manufacturer/research pages. Open Beauty Facts explicitly does not guarantee product-record accuracy. Therefore no product receives a 1–100 score or a 1–5 community rating in this pilot. The database stores each dimension as unavailable with a reason; this is intentional, not a zero score.

## Eligibility and intended methods

- **Ingredients & Safety (1–100):** Do not create a universal safety score. A future score needs verified, current, exact-variant ingredient labeling, product-specific regulatory context, a disclosed hazard/evidence method, and explicit coverage/uncertainty. Ingredient presence alone is not a safety conclusion.
- **Effectiveness (1–100):** Requires evidence about the finished product and intended outcome. Ingredient-level studies are shown separately and cannot be treated as proof that a product works.
- **Transparency (1–100):** Intended to measure how completely a manufacturer discloses traceable product facts, not whether a product is good or safe. A future method should score a fixed published checklist of exact variant, full ingredient label, package size, claim substantiation, and current primary-source links. Community-submitted fields alone do not qualify.
- **Community Experience (1–100):** Requires a sufficiently sized, authentic, lawful review corpus with counts and date range. It remains separate from the star average.
- **Value (1–100):** Requires current, comparable, region-specific price and package size from a permitted source. It is not calculated from a single unverified price.
- **Community rating (1–5 stars):** Show only an authentic, permitted review source or Liv member reviews with count and source/timeframe. No reviews means no rating.

Every future score must expose its method, evidence links, coverage, confidence, and update date. Stale, conflicting, ambiguous, or missing evidence blocks a score rather than lowering it by assumption. Never imply universal safety or a personal health guarantee.
